Interactive Stories

Interactive stories can encourage reflection, creativity, and conversations around important social issues. In this project, older adults aged 65+ learned how to create their own digital interactive stories about ageism using Microsoft PowerPoint during a series of in-person workshops at the University of Manitoba. This website shares the stories created by participants, along with resources for researchers, educators, hobbyists, and anyone curious about interactive storytelling, creativity, aging, and social change.
